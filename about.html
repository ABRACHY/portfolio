I'm Abderrahim — a self-taught frontend developer and digital experience creator based in Morocco.

I got into web development because I was fascinated by the gap between what an idea looks like in someone's head and what actually ends up on screen. That gap is where I work.

I learned by doing: building pages, fixing problems, and slowly developing a sense for what makes a digital product feel right — not just functional, but intentional. Every project I take on is a chance to combine clean code with thoughtful design.

I care about performance, accessibility, and the kind of details most people don't consciously notice — but definitely feel.

Philosophy Build with intention. Ship with clarity.
Approach Design-first, code-informed, user-centered.
