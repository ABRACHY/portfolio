01 About

I build things
people actually
enjoy using.

I'm Abderrahim — a self-taught frontend developer and digital experience creator based in Morocco.

I got into web development because I was fascinated by the gap between what an idea looks like in someone's head and what actually ends up on screen. That gap is where I work.

I learned by doing: building pages, fixing problems, and slowly developing a sense for what makes a digital product feel right — not just functional, but intentional. Every project I take on is a chance to combine clean code with thoughtful design.

I care about performance, accessibility, and the kind of details most people don't consciously notice — but definitely feel.

Philosophy Build with intention. Ship with clarity.
Approach Design-first, code-informed, user-centered.

04 My Process

How I approach
every project.

01

Understanding

Before writing a single line, I listen. What is the business trying to achieve? Who are the users? What problem does this solve? The answers shape everything that follows.

02

Planning

I define the structure, map the user journey, and think through the technical direction. Good planning means fewer surprises and a final product that actually makes sense.

03

Build

Clean, responsive, and functional. I build with performance and accessibility in mind — because a beautiful site that's slow or broken isn't serving anyone.

04

Refinement

I test across devices, fine-tune the details, and optimize until it feels right. The difference between good and great lives in this stage.
